Groups the payments of a card terminal (punto de venta) for one day into
a single journal item, ready to be matched with the single bank
transaction deposited by the bank.

A terminal closing collects every pending payment of a bank journal and
date, optionally filtered by payment method. When posted, it creates one
journal entry that:

- reconciles the outstanding line of each payment, keeping the link
  between the closing and every payment;
- leaves one open line on the outstanding account for the closing total,
  netting refunds even when they use another outstanding account.

That open line is the one to match with the bank statement line.

Use Cases / Context
===================

In Venezuela, card terminals (puntos de venta) do not credit each sale
separately. At the end of the day the terminal runs a closing (cierre de
lote) and the bank deposits the total as a single transaction.

Odoo ends up with many payments (one per sale or per POS session) and
only one bank line, which makes bank reconciliation slow and error
prone. This module turns those payments into a single traceable item
that matches the bank transaction one to one.

Usage
=====

1. Go to **Invoicing > Customers > Terminal Closings** and click
   **New**.
2. Select the bank **Journal** and the **Date** of the terminal closing
   (date of the payments). Set the **Bank Credit Date** when the bank
   credits the money on a later day; the journal entry uses that date so
   it matches the bank transaction.
3. Optionally select the **Payment Methods** of the terminal. Leave it
   empty to include every payment method of the journal.
4. Click **Search Payments**. The closing loads the posted payments of
   that journal and date not yet matched with the bank nor included in
   another closing.
5. Remove any payment that does not belong to the closing with the cross
   icon on its line.
6. Check the **Outstanding Account**. It defaults to the outstanding
   account with the highest amount, usually the one of the customer
   receipts.
7. Click **Post**. A journal entry is created with one open line for the
   closing total.
8. Match that line with the bank statement line as usual.

Each payment shows its **Terminal Closing**, and payments can be
filtered or grouped by closing. A payment that belongs to a posted
closing cannot be reset to draft or cancelled; reset the closing first.
A closing already matched with the bank cannot be reset until the bank
line is unreconciled.
