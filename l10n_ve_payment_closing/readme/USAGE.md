1. Go to **Invoicing > Customers > Terminal Closings** and click **New**.
2. Select the bank **Journal** and the **Date** of the terminal closing
   (date of the payments). Set the **Bank Credit Date** when the bank
   credits the money on a later day; the journal entry uses that date
   so it matches the bank transaction.
3. Optionally select the **Payment Methods** of the terminal. Leave it
   empty to include every payment method of the journal.
4. Click **Search Payments**. The closing loads the posted payments of
   that journal and date not yet matched with the bank nor included in
   another closing.
5. Remove any payment that does not belong to the closing with the
   cross icon on its line.
6. Check the **Outstanding Account**. It defaults to the outstanding
   account with the highest amount, usually the one of the customer
   receipts.
7. Click **Post**. A journal entry is created with one open line for
   the closing total.
8. Match that line with the bank statement line as usual.

Each payment shows its **Terminal Closing**, and payments can be
filtered or grouped by closing. A payment that belongs to a posted
closing cannot be reset to draft or cancelled; reset the closing first.
A closing already matched with the bank cannot be reset until the bank
line is unreconciled.
