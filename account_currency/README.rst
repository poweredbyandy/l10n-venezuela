Shows and keeps invoice amounts in the company currency when the invoice
is issued in another currency:

- Exchange rate of the invoice, its inverse rate and a warning with a
  *Recalcular tasa* button when the rate no longer matches the rate of
  the invoice date.
- Credit notes keep the exchange rate of the invoice they reverse.
- *Subtotal in Company Currency* on every invoice line. It can be edited
  by hand; the line exchange rate is then derived from it and lines
  whose rate differs from the invoice rate are highlighted and can be
  reset.
- Invoice totals widget with an extra column in the company currency.
- Chatter on currencies and exchange rates. Changes of the rate are
  logged in the chatter of the exchange rate.

Use Cases / Context
===================

In Venezuela invoices are often issued in a foreign currency, but the
legal amounts must also be shown in the company currency, with the
exchange rate used. These features used to live inside
``l10n_ve_seniat``, and the manual subtotal in company currency lived in
``currency_account``. They are now a separate module so that the amounts
and rates are computed in one place, while ``l10n_ve_seniat`` keeps the
fiscal rules of the documents.

Databases that already had ``l10n_ve_seniat`` or ``currency_account``
keep their stored values: when this module is installed, it takes
ownership of the existing fields and views instead of creating new ones.

Usage
=====

1. Create an invoice in a currency other than the company currency.
2. The exchange rate of the invoice date is used. Below it, the inverse
   rate is shown.
3. The *Subtotal in Company Currency* column shows each line in the
   company currency. Edit it to force a different amount; the line turns
   red if its rate differs from the invoice rate. Click *Reset* to go
   back to the invoice rate.
4. The totals show an extra column with the amounts in the company
   currency.
5. If the rate of the invoice date changes while the invoice is in
   draft, a warning appears. Click *Recalcular tasa* to apply the
   current rate.

To review the changes made to an exchange rate, open the rate and check
its chatter.
