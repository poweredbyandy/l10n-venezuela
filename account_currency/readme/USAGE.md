1. Create an invoice in a currency other than the company currency.
2. The exchange rate of the invoice date is used. Below it, the inverse rate
   is shown. On vendor bills and refunds in draft both the document rate
   and the inverse rate are editable; writing one updates the other.
   Customer invoices keep them locked when SENIAT is installed.
3. The *Unit Price in Company Currency* column shows the unit price of each
   line in the company currency, before discount.
4. *Subtotal in Company Currency* and *Total in Company Currency* show the
   line amounts at the invoice exchange rate. In draft they are editable:
   changing either one keeps the foreign amounts and adjusts the line rate so
   the company balance matches what you typed. Use *Reset* to go back to the
   invoice rate.
5. The totals show an extra column with the amounts in the company currency.
6. If the rate of the invoice date changes while the invoice is in draft, a
   warning appears. Click *Recalcular tasa* to apply the current rate.

To review the changes made to an exchange rate, open the rate and check its
chatter.
