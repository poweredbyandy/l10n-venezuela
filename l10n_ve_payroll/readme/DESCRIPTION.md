Venezuelan payroll localization on OCA payroll.

Includes legal contribution rates (SSO, FAOV, RPE, INCES, CEPP), social benefit
accruals, contract withholdings, and six salary structures ported from RT:

* Bi-weekly (`BIWEEKLY`)
* Weekly (`WEEKLY`)
* Meal allowance (`MEAL`)
* Leave / vacation (`LEAVE`)
* Profit sharing (`PROFIT`)
* Severance (`SEVERANCE`)

Wage is always in the contract currency; company currency is VES. Exchange rate
on the payslip converts line totals to company currency when needed.

Ships LOTTT working-time calendars (diurnal 40 h, mixed 37.5 h, nocturnal 35 h)
with timezone America/Caracas for use on employees and contracts.
