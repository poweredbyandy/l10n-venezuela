Venezuelan payroll localization on OCA payroll.

Includes legal contribution rates (SSO, FAOV, RPE, INCES, CEPP), social
benefit accruals, contract withholdings, and six salary structures
ported from RT:

- Bi-weekly (``BIWEEKLY``)
- Weekly (``WEEKLY``)
- Meal allowance (``MEAL``)
- Leave / vacation (``LEAVE``)
- Profit sharing (``PROFIT``)
- Severance (``SEVERANCE``)

Wage is always in the contract currency; company currency is VES.
Exchange rate on the payslip converts line totals to company currency
when needed.

Ships LOTTT working-time calendars (diurnal 40 h, mixed 37.5 h,
nocturnal 35 h) with timezone America/Caracas for use on employees and
contracts.

===================

Complements account_currency / SENIAT; does not replace RT or BW
Enterprise modules.\\n

Usage
=====

1. Set the company currency to VES and fill Venezuela Payroll rates in
   Settings (rates are per company; VE structures and salary rules are
   shared / ``company_id`` empty so every company can use them).

2. On the employee/contract, choose a LOTTT working-time calendar
   installed by this module (Settings → Employees → Working Times, or
   contract Working Hours):

   - **VE LOTTT - Jornada diurna (40 h/sem)**: Mon–Fri 08:00–12:00 /
     13:00–17:00
   - **VE LOTTT - Jornada mixta (37,5 h/sem)**: Mon–Fri 7.5 h/day
   - **VE LOTTT - Jornada nocturna (35 h/sem)**: Mon–Fri 00:00–07:00 (7
     h/day)

   On each working-time calendar, open the tab **Non-working paid days**
   and list weekdays that are not worked but still count for salary (day
   + payslip code). VE calendars ship with Saturday=\ ``DDESS`` and
   Sunday=\ ``DDESD``. Payslip create/refresh adds those Worked Days
   lines automatically.

3. Assign an employee a contract with a VE structure (e.g. Quincenal /
   Bi-weekly) and the wage in the contract currency (VES, USD or EUR).

4. Create a payslip for the period; the exchange rate is filled when the
   contract currency differs from the company currency.

5. Compute the sheet to generate assignment, deduction and employer
   contribution lines (SSO, FAOV, RPE, INCES, ISLR, loans, etc.).

6. Confirm the payslip. Structures marked to process social benefits can
   accrue prestaciones on done.
