1. Create one Point of Sale payment method per card terminal.
2. Close the POS sessions as usual. Odoo creates the bank payments of
   each payment method.
3. In **Invoicing > Customers > Terminal Closings**, select the journal,
   the date and the **POS Payment Methods** of the terminal.
4. Click **Search Payments** and then **Post**.

When both **Payment Methods** and **POS Payment Methods** are set,
payments matching any of them are included.
