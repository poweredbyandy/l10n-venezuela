Applies the Venezuelan tax rules to products of companies whose fiscal
country is Venezuela:

- A product created without taxes gets the default sales and purchase taxes
  of the company, or the exempt taxes when the company has none.
- Each product must have exactly one sales tax and one purchase tax per
  Venezuelan company.
- On shared products, a company that has no tax yet gets its default tax.
- Taxes of companies that are not active in the session are kept when the
  taxes are replaced.
