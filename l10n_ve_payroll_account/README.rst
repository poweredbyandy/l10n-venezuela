Links Venezuelan OCA payroll rules to SENIAT chart accounts (nómina por
pagar, IVSS, FAOV, INCES, sueldos, aportes patronales) and provides
provision helpers.

Auto-configures through ``account.chart.template`` for ``ve_seniat``,
``ve_seniat_basic`` and ``ve_seniat_empty``.

Includes a **Register Payment** wizard on payslips/batches to record
employee payouts against account ``2103020`` from a bank or cash
journal.

Usage
=====

Install after ``l10n_ve_payroll`` and ``l10n_ve_seniat``.

On install/upgrade (and when loading a SENIAT chart of accounts), the
module:

1. Ensures payroll liability/expense accounts exist (SENIAT codes such
   as ``2103020``, ``5102001``, ``2103003``\ …) and receivable
   ``1106002`` Prestamos a empleados.
2. Maps salary rule debit/credit accounts from those codes (CRED /
   INTCRED credit ``1106002``).
3. Sets **Settings → Payroll → Loan receivable account** on the company
   (default ``1106002``). Disbursement uses that account; it is not
   chosen per loan.

- Chart ``ve_seniat``: uses accounts from the official CoA CSV.
- Charts ``ve_seniat_basic`` / ``ve_seniat_empty``: creates the same
  payroll accounts via ``account.chart.template`` and maps the rules.

Confirm payslip (accrual)
-------------------------

Confirm a payslip to generate the journal entry (expenses / withholdings
vs ``2103020`` Nómina por pagar).

Register payment (cash out)
---------------------------

1. Open a confirmed payslip (or a batch / select several payslips).
2. Click **Register Payment**.
3. Choose the **bank or cash journal** (where the money left), payment
   date and amounts (partial payments allowed). The default amount is
   the **2103020 balance from the payslip journal entry** (not salary
   rule ``TOTAL``), so multicurrency rounding of Bs 0.01 does not leave
   the payable open.
4. Confirm: creates a posted entry ``Dr 2103020 / Cr bank-cash`` and
   marks the payslip as paid when the residual is zero.

Social security liabilities (``210300x``) are paid separately to each
agency.
