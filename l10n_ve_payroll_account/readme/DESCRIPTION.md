Links Venezuelan OCA payroll rules to SENIAT chart accounts (nómina por pagar,
IVSS, FAOV, INCES, sueldos, aportes patronales) and provides provision helpers.

Auto-configures through `account.chart.template` for `ve_seniat`,
`ve_seniat_basic` and `ve_seniat_empty`.

Includes a **Register Payment** wizard on payslips/batches to record employee
payouts against account `2103020` from a bank or cash journal.
